45. You Can Make Cheese

1Things you need. 2 cups of milk. 4 teaspoons of lemon juice. Or 4 teaspoons of vinegar.

2A pinch of salt. Cheesecloth. Or a thin washcloth. A strainer. A wooden spoon. A stainless steel pot.

3Plastic wrap. Do you want to make cheese? Please ask an adult to help.

4Put the milk into a pot. The helper moves the pot to the burner. The helper turns on the stove.

5Into the milk, stir the salt. Do not stop until the milk bubbles.

6The helper turns off the stove. Into the pot, stir the lemon juice. Use cloth to line a strainer.

7Wait for ten minutes. Curds are the chunks. In the pot, the curds will become cheese.

8Into the strainer, empty the pot. Whey is the liquid. Drain that whey out.

9In the cloth, press the curds. You make a ball from the curds.

10Wrap up the ball. It goes in the fridge. Yum! Cheese is what you made.

46. Your New Plant Talks

1I am a new plant for you. Thank you for taking me home.

2Please give a pot to me. Then please be patient.

3Please give soil to me. Then please be patient.

4Please give water to me. Then please be patient.

5Please give sunlight to me. Then please be patient.

6Please give company to me. Then please be patient. Please give attention to me.

7Then please be patient. Give all these things to me.

8Something will happen then. I will not talk.

9I will not sing. Magic tricks are not for me.

10Be patient with me. Then I will grow.
